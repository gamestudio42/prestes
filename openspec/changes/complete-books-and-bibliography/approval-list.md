# Approval list: quotations and images

The owner approves this list before anything goes into the game (task 1.1).
Each row says what was checked and where. Nothing marked **pending** ships
until the owner closes it.

Legend:

- **Checked:** the wording was read in a file in this repository or in
  `~/Downloads`, at the page given.
- **Secondary:** read only as another author quotes it. The original was not
  seen.
- **Pending:** cannot ship until the owner, or a later research pass,
  confirms the original wording, edition or page.
- **Rights:** `PD` means public domain in Brazil: the author died more than
  70 years ago (Lei 9.610, art. 41), or the photograph was published more
  than 70 years ago (art. 44). `free` means a free licence. `citation` means
  the work is still protected and is quoted briefly, with attribution,
  under art. 46, III. A `citation` quote is capped at 40 words and needs an
  edition and a page.
