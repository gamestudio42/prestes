## 7. Bridges that read as bridges (asked by the user while authoring, 2026-11-09)

The user's screenshot shows a wooden bridge over a deep river that still reads as an embankment or causeway:
- sandy ground fills the span under the deck, and the water stops at the deck's footprint;
- the deck looks like stepped blocks of light and dark;
- the ends sink into the banks like a mound.

Found in code (not yet confirmed by a repro):
- `packages/shared/src/maps/runtime.ts` (about line 265): every cell inside `bridgePolygon` gets `water = 0` and surface `'bridge'`, so the ground under the deck is baked as dry ground; `scene-view.ts` `surfaceColor` paints `'bridge'` as `PALETTE.riverbed`, which is the sandy patch.
- `document.ts` (about line 950): the ground under the span is only lowered to `deck - BRIDGE_CLEARANCE` (0.4 m). Where the bank is high or the bridge is long, the ground stays near deck height, so the bridge reads as a fill.
- `battle-map-layer.ts` `bridge()`: the deck is 1 m boxes, each tilted on its own (`DECK_PIECE`), with rails per piece. Flat-shaded pieces at slightly different tilts shade differently, which gives the stepped look. The ramps (`BRIDGE_RAMP` 3 m) run the deck down into the bank.
