## 4. Bridges and smooth edges

- [x] 4.1 Bridge abutments: deck heights from the landings, ground lowered under the span on authored maps, the same heights for walking (verify: unit test "deck over a high bank"; screenshot shows no ground through the deck; hand-written goldens checked)
  - Apply note: packages/shared/src/maps/runtime.ts: bridgeDeckHeight(bridge, waterLevel, ends, along) is the one deck profile: each end at its landing's ground, the core the higher of waterLevel + deckHeight and the line between the ends, smooth ramps of BRIDGE_RAMP = 3 m (at most a quarter of the span). runtimeHeightAt uses it through runtimeBridgeDeckAt (landings cached per runtime). compileMapDocument lowers the ground between the ramps (and 2 m either side of the deck) to 0.4 m under the deck; landings are sampled from the field before the lowering and match the runtime's. - [x] 4.2 River centrelines smoothed (Catmull-Rom, 2 m) at compile time; water outlines by marching squares at 0.5 m (verify: unit test "diagonal river bank" has no 1 m staircase in the outline; `fazenda-doc` golden re-recorded if it changes)
  - Apply note: packages/shared/src/maps/water-outline.ts: smoothCentreline(points) is a centripetal Catmull-Rom through the corner points, cut into pieces of equal arc length of at most RIVER_STEP = 2 m, each piece keeping its source segment. compileMapDocument carves the river and builds each segment's water strip from the smoothed points (a ford keeps its kind and its crossing ends at the source segment's middle); the growth mask uses the same smoothed line. traceWaterOutlines(map, areas, subtract) runs marching squares on a 0.5 m grid of the signed distance to the water's edge (crossings interpolated, saddles decided by the cell centre, the grid one node past the map so every loop closes) and simplifies each loop by Douglas-Peucker at 5 cm; outlineShapes pairs outer loops with their holes. The view draws deep water as one mesh inside those outlines ('water:deep') and the fords' river reaches as another minus the deep water ('water:ford'); ford polygons keep their own look. Hand-written maps keep their centrelines (their strips are traced, not re-smoothed). Unit tests in packages/shared/test/water-outline.test.ts (diagonal river bank at 30 degrees: walking the outline every 25 cm stays within 6 cm of the bank line; bent centreline; ford reach; pond with an island) and packages/view3d/test/battle-map-water.test.ts (Serra-rio). fazenda-doc has no rivers, so its golden holds; all goldens unchanged. Serra-rio traces in about 50 ms.
- [x] 4.3 Dithered material borders in `PaintedGround` (verify: pixel-art snapshot re-recorded after review; shader unit test of the Bayer offset at a border cell)
