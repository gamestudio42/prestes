# Request to Duke University Press (draft, in English)

- **To:** Duke University Press, Rights and Permissions. Check the address
  and the form on the Press's site.
- **Subject:** Permission request: two maps from Jacob Blanc, *The Prestes
  Column* (2024), for a free educational game

The book is CC BY-NC 4.0, but its maps are credited "Courtesy of Gabe Moss".
That credit may mean the maps are outside the book's licence, so this
request asks.

---

Dear Rights and Permissions team,

I am developing *Prestes*, a free, open-source (AGPL-3.0), non-commercial
educational browser game about the Prestes Column (1922–1927). It has no
sales and no advertising. The game includes a campaign book and a historical
compendium in Portuguese and English. Every fact in them is sourced, and the
compendium ends with a full bibliography in which Jacob Blanc's *The Prestes
Column: An Interior History of Brazil* (Duke University Press, 2024) is a
main source.

I would like permission to reproduce and adapt two maps from the book:

- Map 2.1, "Rebel escape from the Siege of São Luís", for the chapter on the
  breakout from São Luiz Gonzaga (December 1924).
- Map 5.1, "Prestes Column in Bahia, April–July 1926", for the chapter on
  Serra Nova.

Each map would be reduced to about 320 × 240 pixels in the game's sepia
palette, with labels redrawn in Portuguese and English. It would carry the
credit "Map by Gabe Moss, from Jacob Blanc, *The Prestes Column* (Duke
University Press, 2024)", or any credit you specify. The game will be
published free of charge on the web.

Could you also tell me whether the maps fall under the book's CC BY-NC 4.0
licence, or whether I should write to Gabe Moss directly?
