# Historical Sources

This is a working bibliography, not a claim that every entry has been read in
full. Consultation status is explicit.

## Core sources

### `macaulay-1974` — Tier 0

Neill Macaulay, *The Prestes Column: Revolution in Brazil* (New York: New
Viewpoints / Franklin Watts, 1974). ISBN 0-531-05356-3 (hardcover),
0-531-05563-9 (paperback).

Status: **full text layer extracted** from the local DRM-free copy. Text:
`livros/macaulay/text/macaulay.txt`; chapter files under
`livros/macaulay/text/chapters/`; frontispiece map rendered to
`livros/macaulay/maps/page-006.png`. See `MACAULAY_NOTES.md`.

Authority: chronology, route, order of battle and military operations.

### `blanc-2024` — Tier 1 social history

Jacob Blanc, *The Prestes Column: An Interior History of Modern Brazil*
(Durham: Duke University Press, 2024). ISBN 978-1-4780-2582-5.

Brazilian edition: *A Coluna Prestes: uma história do interior* (São Paulo:
UNESP, 2024), translated by Laiz Rubinger Chen Ferguson.

Status: **local copy**. Authority: social and interior history, civilians,
local politics, legitimacy, memory.

### `amado-1942` — Tier 1 tone

Jorge Amado, *O Cavaleiro da Esperança: vida de Luiz Carlos Prestes* (1942).

Status: **citation-only**. Authority: tone, narrative arc and the "Knight of
Hope" myth. Not a factual authority. No verbatim reproduction.

  a Revolução de Outubro* (Pelotas: Livraria do Globo, 1931); later edition
  *A Coluna Prestes: marchas e combates* (São Paulo: Afa-Omega, 1979).

- `tavora-1928` — Juarez Távora, *À guisa de depoimento sobre a Revolução
  Brasileira de 1924* (São Paulo: O Combate, 1928). Status: citation-only.
