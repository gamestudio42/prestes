# Historical Sources

This is a working bibliography, not a claim that every entry has been read in
full. Consultation status is explicit.

## Core sources

### `macaulay-1974` — Tier 0

Neill Macaulay, _The Prestes Column: Revolution in Brazil_ (New York: New
Viewpoints / Franklin Watts, 1974). ISBN 0-531-05356-3 (hardcover),
0-531-05563-9 (paperback).

Status: **full text layer extracted** from the local DRM-free copy. Text:
`livros/macaulay/text/macaulay.txt`; chapter files under
`livros/macaulay/text/chapters/`; frontispiece map rendered to
`livros/macaulay/maps/page-006.png`. See `MACAULAY_NOTES.md`.

Authority: chronology, route, order of battle and military operations.

### `blanc-2024` — Tier 1 social history

Jacob Blanc, _The Prestes Column: An Interior History of Modern Brazil_
(Durham: Duke University Press, 2024). ISBN 978-1-4780-2582-5.

Brazilian edition: _A Coluna Prestes: uma história do interior_ (São Paulo:
UNESP, 2024), translated by Laiz Rubinger Chen Ferguson.

Status: **local copy**. Authority: social and interior history, civilians,
local politics, legitimacy, memory. Chapter 7 ("Constructing the Knight of
Hope") and fig. 7.5 record the La Gaiba monument and its inscription, "Glory to
the Bandeirantes of Freedom, 1924-1927".

### `amado-1942` — Tier 1 tone

Jorge Amado, _O Cavaleiro da Esperança: vida de Luiz Carlos Prestes_ (1942).

Status: **citation-only**. Authority: tone, narrative arc and the "Knight of
Hope" myth. Not a factual authority. No verbatim reproduction.

## Finding aids

- `mvcp-portal` — Portal Anticolonialismo / Memorial Virtual Coluna Prestes
  (UFU), <https://www.anticolonialismo.org/coluna-prestes>. Accessed 30 Oct. 2026. Tier 4: finding aid, not authority. The portal has an explicit
  anticolonial perspective; its bibliography and open repositories are useful,
  but citations point to the underlying works.
- `cpdoc-coluna` — CPDOC/FGV, "Coluna Prestes", verbete da Primeira República,
  <https://cpdoc.fgv.br/sites/default/files/verbetes/primeira-republica/COLUNA%20PRESTES.pdf>.
  Accessed 6 Nov. 2026. Tier 3: reference article with a bibliography; useful
  for the La Gaiba/Libres split and the exile network, not for page-level
  chronology.
- `cpdoc-prestes` — CPDOC/FGV, "Luis Carlos Prestes", Atlas Histórico do Brasil,
  <https://atlas.fgv.br/verbetes/luis-carlos-prestes>. Accessed 6 Nov. 2026.
  Tier 3: reference biography with a bibliography; authority for Prestes's
  family background, military formation, 1922–1927 trajectory and later
  political life. It is a summary, not a substitute for the cited biographies.
- `almg-2026` — Assembleia Legislativa de Minas Gerais, "Obra sobre a Coluna
  Prestes no Norte de Minas é lançada na ALMG", 10 Apr. 2026,
  <https://www.almg.gov.br/comunicacao/noticias/arquivos/Obra-sobre-a-Coluna-Prestes-no-Norte-de-Minas-e-lancada-na-ALMG/>.
  Accessed 6 Nov. 2026. Tier 4: launch note and summary; cite the underlying
  book (`nascimento-2026`) when possible.
- `fapesp-2007` — Gonçalo Junior, "A revolução que São Paulo esqueceu",
  _Pesquisa FAPESP_ 135 (May 2007), review of `cohen-2007`,
  <https://revistapesquisa.fapesp.br/bombas-sobre-sao-paulo-a-revolucao-de-1924/>.
  Accessed 6 Nov. 2026. Tier 4: review; use for the bombarded districts and
  the civilian experience, with `cohen-2007` as the underlying authority.
- `revolucao-cultural-2025` — Revolução Cultural, "SC: cidade de Descanso
  comemora os 100 anos da passagem da Coluna Prestes por suas terras",
  <https://www.revolucaocultural.com.br/post/sc-cidade-de-descanso-comemora-os-100-anos-da-passagem-da-coluna-prestes-por-suas-terras>.
  Accessed 6 Nov. 2026. Tier 4: local memory and date confirmation for Ramada
  (3 Jan. 1925); not a page-level authority.
