# 07 - O Pantanal

## Pitch

February 1927. The column enters the Pantanal, exhausted and short of everything.
The government is behind it, the water is ahead, and the border is the only
objective left. The player screens the column through marsh, water and cattle
trails, keeps the men together, and reaches the border. This is not a battle for
ground; it is a battle against the march itself.

Target length: 15-20 minutes at 1x, in one sitting.

## Historical frame

In January-February 1927 the column crossed the Paraguay River near its
headwaters and swung south into the Pantanal, losing most of its horses and at
times marching in water. The main body entered Bolivia at San Matias on 3
February 1927; Siqueira Campos’s group crossed into Paraguay in March. The march
dissolved into exile. Macaulay covers the road in ch. IX, pp. 230–234; the page-
level detail for the exact crossing points is still thin.

### Historical, dramatized, fictional

- **Historical:** the column's crossing of the Pantanal in January-February
  1927; the Paraguay/Bolivia split; the march dissolving into exile.
- **Dramatized:** the marsh route, the crossings, the fatigue and the dialogue.
- **Fictional:** the individual soldiers and the exact route. The Pantanal map is
  a game abstraction.

## Perspective

Luís Carlos Prestes, chief of staff, leading the column through the Pantanal. The
player sees the end of the march from the command side: a column that is still
moving, a government that is still behind it, and a landscape that offers no
cover and no rest.

## Player fantasy and pillar

- **Fantasy:** Get the column through the Pantanal and across the border, knowing
  the march is ending.
- **Pillar:** 3, the march costs more than the battle; 1, command the section, not
  the hero; 4, history with receipts.
- **Loop beat:** Sustain. The mission changes the Sustain beat: water, fatigue,
  supply and the wounded matter more than fire.

## Objectives

### Primary

- Cross the marsh and the water.
- Keep the column together.
- Bring the wounded.
- Reach the border.
- Avoid or delay the government patrols.

### Optional

- Save the supply animals.
- Keep the sections in cohesion.
- Find a guide or a cattle trail.
- Reach the border with the column's records.

### Failure

- The column is lost in the marsh.
- The wounded are abandoned.
- The government cuts the route.
- Prestes falls or is captured.
- The column disperses before the border.

## Map and terrain

- The Pantanal, February-March 1927. Marsh, water, islands, cattle trails, a river
  and the border.
- The government patrols the roads; the column moves through water and marsh. The
  heat, the fatigue and the supply are the main threats.
- Cover: none worth the name; the cattle trails, the islands and the riverbanks
  are the only ground. The water is slow and exhausting.
- The map teaches the cost of the march: fatigue, supply and cohesion. The real
  Pantanal route is a source abstraction.

## Beats

### Briefing

The Pantanal, February 1927. The column is exhausted; the border is ahead. Prestes
tells the rearguard to keep the column together, bring the wounded and reach the
border. There is no battle to win; the march is the objective.

### Opening

The column enters the marsh. The water and the heat slow it. The first patrol is
sighted.

### Mid-mission

The column strings out. The wounded and the supply animals fall behind. The player
chooses what to carry and what to leave, and keeps the sections together.

### Climax

The border is ahead. The last water crossing. The patrols close. The player gets
the column across and reaches the border.

### Debrief

The main body enters Bolivia; Siqueira’s group enters Paraguay. The march
dissolves into exile. The cost is counted: men, animals, supplies and the miles
behind them.

## Victory and defeat

- **Victory:** the column reaches the border with its wounded and enough of its
  men and records to continue. The march is over; the exile begins.
- **Defeat:** the column is lost in the marsh, the wounded are abandoned, the
  patrols cut the route, Prestes falls or is captured, or the column disperses.
  Retry from deployment.
- The debrief must state the cost: the column survives, but the march is ending.
