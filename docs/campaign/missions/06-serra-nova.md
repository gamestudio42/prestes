# 06 - Serra Nova

## Pitch

April 1926. The column is in northern Minas Gerais, and the rearguard is
attacked at Serra Nova. The men are exhausted, the jagunços are on the rear, and
the federal troops are trying to close the ring. The player commands the
rearguard, breaks contact, covers the column’s turn, and gets it through
Taiobeiras. There is no victory here; there is the next march.

Target length: 15-20 minutes at 1x, in one sitting.

## Historical frame

On 22 April 1926 the column’s rearguard was attacked at Serra Nova, Minas Gerais,
by the jagunços of Horácio de Matos and Abílio Wolney. The column broke contact
and threw the pursuers off; the federal troops tried to close the ring, and the
escape was remembered as the “laço húngaro,” a series of circular movements. The
column passed Taiobeiras on 26 April. Macaulay’s frontispiece map divides the
march at Serra Nova for cartographic convenience; it does not mean a “second
phase” began there.

### Historical, dramatized, fictional

- **Historical:** the rearguard attack at Serra Nova on 22 April 1926; the
  jagunço pursuit; the break of contact; the federal attempt to encircle the
  column; the “laço húngaro”; the passage through Taiobeiras on 26 April.
- **Dramatized:** the exact maneuvers, the rearguard positions, the timing and
  the dialogue.
- **Fictional:** the individual soldiers and the exact route. The highland map is
  a game abstraction.

## Perspective

Luís Carlos Prestes, chief of staff, covering the column’s escape from the
Serra Nova encirclement. The player sees the rearguard from the command side: a
column that is still alive, jagunços and federal troops behind it, and a
landscape of ridges, roads and scrub.

## Player fantasy and pillar

- **Fantasy:** Keep an exhausted column moving through the highlands and reach
  Serra Nova before the pursuit closes.
- **Pillar:** 1, command the section, not the hero; 2, read the ground; 3, the
  march costs more than the battle.
- **Loop beat:** Sustain. The mission changes the Sustain beat: fatigue, supply
  and the column's cohesion matter more than a firefight.

## Objectives

### Primary

- Hold the Serra Nova rearguard long enough for the column to turn.
- Break contact with the jagunços.
- Cover the column’s change of direction.
- Reach Taiobeiras on 26 April.
- Keep the column together.

### Optional

- Bring the wounded.
- Save the supply animals.
- Delay the federal pursuit.
- Keep the sections in cohesion.

### Failure

- The column is cut at Serra Nova.
- The rearguard cannot break contact.
- The rearguard is destroyed.
- Prestes falls or is captured.
- The column disperses.

## Map and terrain

- Serra Nova and Taiobeiras, northern Minas Gerais, April 1926. Ridges, roads,
  scrub, woods and open high ground.
- The column turns; the jagunços follow; the federal troops try to close the
  roads ahead. The mission is a break of contact and a maneuver, not a pass
  crossing.
- Cover: rocks, woods, road cuts and ridges. The open high ground is exposed;
  the roads are the pursuit’s advantage.
- The map teaches the cost of a march: fatigue, supply, cohesion and the
  maneuver that keeps a column alive. The real route is a source abstraction.

## Beats

### Briefing

Serra Nova, April 1926. The rearguard is attacked; the column must turn. Prestes
tells the rearguard to hold long enough for the column to break contact, then
follow it toward Taiobeiras.

### Opening

The jagunços probe the rear. The federal troops press the roads. The player
chooses where to hold and where to give ground.

### Mid-mission

The column turns. The rearguard trades ground and time, keeping the pursuers
looking in the wrong direction. The player rotates the sections and keeps the
column together.

### Climax

The column is away. The rearguard breaks contact and follows. The “laço húngaro”
is the memory of the maneuver, not a set-piece battle.

### Debrief

The column is away from Serra Nova and through Taiobeiras. The cost is counted:
fatigue, supplies, men. The march continues toward the border.
