# 05 - A Ponte de Verdinho

## Pitch

The column has crossed the Verdinho, but the bridge is the only dry crossing.
Prestes must hold it until the wagons are through, then burn it and get the
rearguard across the shallow river before the pursuit arrives. The pursuers will
cut a ford with the bridge's own timbers. The mission is a rearguard, a
demolition and a river crossing, not a pitched battle.

Target length: 15-20 minutes at 1x, in one sitting.

## Historical frame

On 27-28 June 1925, at the Verdinho bridge outside Mineiros, Goiás, the column’s
main body crossed on 27 June. Prestes and Cordeiro’s rearguard detachment held
the bridge that afternoon, stopped Klinger’s advance, and burned the bridge. The
rearguard held the west bank overnight and waded the shallow river on the morning
of 28 June. Klinger later cut a ford into the banks with the bridge’s timbers.
The action delayed the government and kept the column moving north.

## Perspective

Luís Carlos Prestes, chief of staff, directing the rearguard while the column
crosses. The player sees the bridge as a clock: every minute the rearguard holds
is a minute the column gains, and every minute too long is a minute the
government gains. Cordeiro commands the rearguard detachment at the bridge;
Prestes is the command voice and is present.

## Player fantasy and pillar

- **Fantasy:** Hold a bridge long enough to save the column, then destroy it and
  get the rearguard across the river.
- **Pillar:** 1, command the section, not the hero; 2, read the ground; 3, the
  march costs more than the battle.
- **Loop beat:** Decide. The mission changes the Decide beat: the bridge must be
  held and then given up at the right moment.

## Objectives

### Primary

- Hold the Verdinho bridge until the column's wagons are across.
- Prepare and burn the bridge.
- Withdraw the rearguard through the ford.
- Prevent the government from crossing on the bridge.

### Optional

- Bring the wounded across.
- Keep the rearguard together.
- Delay the pursuit after the burning.
- Recover what can be carried from the bridge stores.

### Failure

- The government takes the bridge before the column is across.
- The bridge is burned too early, trapping the rearguard.
- The ford is lost.
- Prestes falls or is captured.
- The column is cut.

## Map and terrain

- The Verdinho bridge, Mineiros, Goiás, 27-28 June 1925. A road bridge over a
  shallow river, with wooded banks, a ford and open ground on the approaches.
- The column is on the far bank; the government comes from the near bank. The
  bridge is the only dry crossing; the ford is the escape.
- Cover: the bridge structure, the banks, the woods and the riverbed. The open
  road is dangerous; the bridge is a chokepoint.
- The map teaches the cost of denial: burning the bridge saves the column and
  gives the pursuers the timbers for a ford. The real bridge is a source
  abstraction.

## Beats

### Briefing

The column has crossed the Verdinho. The bridge is the only dry crossing. Prestes
tells the rearguard to hold until the wagons are through, then burn the bridge
and leave by the ford. The player is told that the bridge is not a position to
keep; it is a position to give up at the right time.

### Opening

The government approaches. The rearguard prepares the bridge and takes the near
bank. The first patrols test the bridge.

### Mid-mission

The wagons cross. The government presses the bridge. The player rotates the
sections, holds the bridge and watches the ford. The wounded and the stores
compete for time.

### Climax

The last wagons are across. The player burns the bridge and withdraws to the
ford. The government reaches the burning bridge and begins to cut a ford with
the timbers. The rearguard crosses the shallow river under fire.

### Debrief

The bridge is down. The column is across the river. The pursuit is delayed, but
not stopped: the timbers will help the government. The column continues north.
The cost is counted.

## Victory and defeat

- **Victory:** the column crosses, the bridge is burned, and the rearguard
  withdraws through the ford. The pursuit is delayed, not stopped.
- **Defeat:** the government takes the bridge before the column is across, the
  bridge is burned too early, the ford is lost, Prestes falls or is captured, or
  the column is cut. Retry from deployment.
- The debrief must state the cost: the bridge is gone, the timbers help the
  enemy, and the column keeps moving.
