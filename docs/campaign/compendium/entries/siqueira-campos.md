# Antônio de Siqueira Campos

## Short body

**EN:** Lieutenant, one of the 18 of the Forte de Copacabana in 1922 and later
a leader of the column. He survived the beach. The game's Prologue is his, not
Prestes's.

**PT-BR:** Tenente, um dos 18 do Forte de Copacabana em 1922 e depois um dos
líderes da coluna. Sobreviveu à praia. O Prólogo do jogo é dele, não de Prestes.

## Extended note

Siqueira Campos is the figure who takes the 18 out of the fort and onto the
beach. The game makes him the Prologue commander because Prestes was absent
with typhoid and because the beach is the moment that enters the legend. The
mission is not a one-man story: the player commands four groups, and the 18 are
the subject. Siqueira is the voice that holds them together.

The legend says the eighteen died on the beach. The record says Siqueira
Campos survived. The compendium keeps both: he is the exception that the
legend remembers, and the man who later carried the 1922 story into the column.
The exact fort command structure is still a source question; the registry makes
him the Prologue commander, and the mission follows that design.

He is not a superhero. The 1922 failure is the beginning of his arc, not a
victory. The campaign uses him to show what a certain defeat costs and what it
leaves behind.
