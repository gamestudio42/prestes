# Batalhões patrióticos / Patriotic Battalions

Cite: Macaulay, pp. 15, 70–71, 189, 192–193, 218–219, 228

## English

### Volunteers and gunmen

Patriotic Battalions were civilian units mustered into federal service. In São
Paulo in 1924 they were volunteers and did badly in the pursuit (Macaulay, pp.
15, 70–71). In the sertão in 1925 and 1926 they were bands of jagunços under
their bosses, given federal uniforms, arms and pay: Franklin de Albuquerque's on
the São Francisco, Horácio de Matos's Diamond Mines Battalion from March 1926
(pp. 189, 218–219).

The theatre commander admitted he had almost no control over them. Siqueira
Campos judged them led without any theory but more effective than regular troops
against the column; they hollowed their bullets to make worse wounds (pp.
192–193, 228).

## Português (BR)

### Voluntários e jagunços

Os batalhões patrióticos eram unidades civis incorporadas ao serviço federal. Em
São Paulo, em 1924, eram voluntários e se saíram mal na perseguição (Macaulay,
pp. 15, 70–71). No sertão, em 1925 e 1926, eram bandos de jagunços sob seus
chefes, com farda, armas e soldo federais: os de Franklin de Albuquerque no São
Francisco, o Batalhão das Lavras Diamantinas de Horácio de Matos a partir de
março de 1926 (pp. 189, 218–219).

O comandante do teatro admitiu que quase não tinha controle sobre eles. Siqueira
Campos os julgava chefiados sem teoria nenhuma, mas mais eficazes que a tropa
regular contra a Coluna; eles escavavam as balas para fazer ferimentos piores
(pp. 192–193, 228).
