# Infantaria do Exército / Infantaria do Exército

Cite: Macaulay, pp. 90–91, 178, 192 (the federal army); a game organization

## English

Federal infantry. The army was the government's regular force, but it shrank in
the mutinies and its men were unreliable against their former comrades; it was
of little use in the Northeast (Macaulay, pp. 90–91, 178, 192). The section is
ours. Part of the legalist forces.

## Português (BR)

Infantaria federal. O Exército era a força regular do governo, mas encolheu com
os levantes e seus soldados não eram confiáveis contra os antigos companheiros;
serviu pouco no Nordeste (Macaulay, pp. 90–91, 178, 192). A seção é nossa. Parte
das forças legalistas.
