# Forte de Copacabana

## Short body

**EN:** The fort in Rio de Janeiro whose garrison rose in July 1922. The rebels
marched out along the beach rather than surrender; government forces overcame
the fort. The beach is the prologue's battlefield.

**PT-BR:** O forte no Rio de Janeiro cuja guarnição se levantou em julho de 1922. Os rebeldes saíram pela praia em vez de se render; as forças do governo
tomaram o forte. A praia é o campo de batalha do prólogo.

## Extended note

The Forte de Copacabana was one of the centers of the 1922 Rio rising. The plan
depended on a coordinated movement that did not happen: the Realengo cadets were
fired on at Vila Militar, and the government closed in on the fort. The garrison
did not wait to be taken. The rebels marched out along the beach and fought
there.

The fort is a place of memory; the beach is the action. The legend of the 18 do
Forte belongs to both. The compendium should describe the fort as a real
fortification and the beach as the ground of the prologue, without pretending
that the game's map is a survey. The real plan, garrison and armament are still
source gaps.

Prestes was not there. The fort is Siqueira Campos's ground; the column will be
Prestes's.
